Prototype speed punishes fuzzy product ownership.

Fast demos feel great until the room has to answer boring questions.

What can the user trust?
Who reviews the AI answer?
Is this workflow product, or still a throwaway test?

My view: most founder-led AI teams need a tighter weekly decision rhythm before they need a heavier product org.

A hire can help later. This week, the team needs one owner and one written boundary:

1. Pick the decision blocking real work.
2. Write the boundary in plain language.
3. Name the evidence that would change it.
4. Put one person on it until next Friday.

Small enough to repeat.

Serious enough to matter.

When the rhythm holds, the future product leader inherits a cleaner map of decisions. When it breaks, you have located the job before you have drafted the job description.

I wrote more about shrinking the AI product surface before the hire: https://www.edgecaser.com/posts/fractional-scope-before-hiring.html
