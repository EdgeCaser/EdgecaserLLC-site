Who should book?

Book when there is an active product call to make, not a loose intro. In 30 minutes, Ian Brillembourg will pressure-test the problem, sort the likely engagement model, and sketch what the first 2 to 4 weeks should prove.

What is this page not for?

Skip the calendar if the ask is pure design staff augmentation, production-only UX help, implementation with no product decision authority, or specialist advice that belongs to legal, security, privacy, or compliance owners.

Will pricing be covered?

Pricing stays at the engagement-model level in v1. Expect a directional fit discussion, such as fractional, sprint-based, review-based, process-focused, implementation-guidance, or advisory. Hourly rates and exact ranges are not published here.

What is included after we work together?

Every engagement includes lifetime access to Shipwright Plus, the private premium version of the open-source Shipwright PM toolkit. Shipwright Plus adds a cross-model conflict harness for high-stakes product decisions, with an optional 30-minute setup call and one year of email support.
