The weakest AI review plan is the orphaned final checkpoint.

A lot of AI product specs still treat human review as a box near the end. I think that is where oversight starts to fail.

Name the job instead.

Who plans the work before the model starts?
Who watches while it runs?
Who approves an action before anything changes?
Who checks the output after the fact?

Those are separate product moments. The UI and the owner should follow the moment, not the word review.

The accept button is too late for every trust decision.

Put the review points in the spec while the workflow is still cheap to move. Design the handoffs around the moments where a person can still change the outcome.
